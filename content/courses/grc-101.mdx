<Card>
  <h2>📘 What is Governance?</h2>
  <p>
    Governance refers to the framework, policies, and processes that guide an organization toward achieving its goals.
    It ensures accountability, transparency, and alignment with strategic objectives.
  </p>
</Card>

<Card>
  <h2>⚠️ What is Risk?</h2>
  <p>
    Risk is the potential for loss, disruption, or negative outcomes. GRC professionals assess, mitigate, and monitor risks across people, processes, and technology.
  </p>
</Card>

<Card>
  <h2>📋 What is Compliance?</h2>
  <p>
    Compliance involves adhering to laws, regulations, and internal policies. It's about proving that you're "doing the right thing" — and documenting it.
  </p>
</Card>
