## 👋 Welcome

**GRC 101** is your first step into the world of Governance, Risk, and Compliance.

If you're curious about how businesses manage uncertainty, meet regulatory expectations, and build trust — this course is for you.

---

## 📌 What You’ll Learn

✅ What **Governance** is and why it matters  
✅ Core principles of **Risk Management**  
✅ The role of **Compliance** in business  
✅ How GRC supports **security, audit, and IT operations**  
✅ How GRC ties into frameworks like ISO 27001, NIST, and SOC 2

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you'll be able to:

- **Define** Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)  
- **Explain** GRC’s role in IT and business  
- **Identify** real-world job roles and responsibilities  
- **Connect** GRC to major frameworks and certifications  
- **Speak confidently** about security and compliance

---

## 👥 Who This Course Is For

This course is ideal for:

- Career changers exploring GRC for the first time  
- IT professionals transitioning into governance or risk  
- Cybersecurity students and entry-level analysts  
- Business pros seeking to understand policy and assurance

---

## 🔗 Next Steps

Once you complete this course, you'll be ready to:

- Build your own **risk register**  
- Draft GRC policies and governance frameworks  
- Conduct **compliance assessments**  
- Map internal controls to frameworks like ISO and SOC 2
